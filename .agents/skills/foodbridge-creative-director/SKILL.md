# FoodBridge Creative Director Skill

## Goal
Provide a clear, repeatable framework for the visual identity, branding, storytelling, and overall design consistency of the FoodBridge website. This skill guides every major design decision to ensure a premium, socially‑impactful experience.

## Instructions

### 1️⃣ Brand Identity
- **Brand**: FoodBridge
- **Tagline**: *Turning Surplus Into Smiles.*
- **Mission**: Reduce food waste and connect surplus food with communities in need.
- **Core Values**: empathy, sustainability, community, transparency.

### 3️⃣ Design Language
- **Editorial premium feel** – clean, readable layouts with strong hierarchy.
- **Social‑impact startup aesthetic** – friendly, hopeful, and trustworthy.
- **Typography** – choose a high‑contrast pairing (e.g., **Montserrat** for headings, **Source Serif Pro** for body). Define a scale from large‑display headings down to body copy and captions.
- **Photography** – showcase beautiful, authentic food imagery; avoid stock‑photo clichés.
- **Components** – elegant cards, grid‑based layouts, generous whitespace, subtle shadows.
- **Micro‑animations** – fade‑in, slide‑up, and hover effects (see Motion Design skill) – keep them minimal and purposeful.

### 4️⃣ User Experience Priorities
- **Primary Action** – make food donation the most prominent CTA on every page.
- **Navigation** – a simple top‑level menu (Home, About, Donate, Stories, Contact) with clear hierarchy.
- **Emotional Connection** – use storytelling snippets, real‑world impact numbers (verified), and testimonial videos.
- **Visual Distinction** – each major section (hero, impact stats, donation flow, community stories) should have a distinct visual treatment while respecting the overall design language.
- **Mobile‑First** – design layouts for mobile first, then expand to tablet and desktop, ensuring all typography and touch targets meet accessibility standards.

### 5️⃣ Quality Control Checklist
- ❌ No generic AI template copy‑pasting.
- ❌ No excessive gradients, glass‑morphism, or over‑styled elements.
- ✅ Consistent spacing (use a base **8 px** grid system).
- ✅ Consistent typography (font families, sizes, line‑height, letter‑spacing).
- ✅ All statistics must be sourced and verifiable; never fabricate data.
- ✅ Testimonials must be clearly marked as *illustrative* if not real.
- ✅ All components pass an accessibility audit (contrast ≥ 4.5:1, focus order, ARIA where needed).
- ✅ Review each major design decision against this document before committing.

## Deliverables
- `brand-identity.md` – brand statement, tagline, mission, values.
- `color-palette.json` – JSON with the palette colors and usage tokens.
- `typography.md` – font families, hierarchy, scale, and CSS variables.
- `design-language.md` – component guidelines, photography style, animation references.
- `ux-principles.md` – primary CTA placement, navigation map, mobile‑first notes.
- `quality-control-checklist.md` – the checklist above for designers and developers.

## Usage
Run this skill whenever a new visual element, component, or layout is proposed. Verify that the proposal aligns with the brand identity, color palette, design language, UX priorities, and quality‑control checklist before moving to implementation.

> **Note:** Do **not** start any website development until the creative direction is fully approved and documented.
